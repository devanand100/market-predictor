VIX, reward-to-risk and "quiet then explode" filters

Three ideas were tested: a VIX entry filter, skipping trades with poor reward-to-risk, and the pattern where a sideways market suddenly makes a big move. Every threshold was set from 2021–2023 data and then checked unchanged on 2024–2026.

Reward-to-risk filter for Strategy 2 Adopted

Skip the trade when the premium collected is under 23.2% of the maximum loss, which is under about 38 points on a 200-point spread. These thin-credit trades earned close to nothing in both periods (−0.07% and −0.02% per trade). Every cutoff from 20% to 28% improved the result, so the benefit does not depend on one exact number.
